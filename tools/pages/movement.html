BC Watershed Security Coalition

The policy alliance

A non-partisan coalition of 57+ organizations working directly with the province on lasting reform: a $1-billion Watershed Security Fund, modern industrial water rates, and local watershed boards. Visit watershedsecurity.ca →

Same mission, different tools: one moves the public, the other moves the policy. You strengthen both every time you take action.

What the coalition is asking of B.C. right now

The 2026 asks

  1. Fully implement the Water Sustainability Act — ten years old and still not delivering its promises.
  2. Implement and resource local watershed boards — so drought decisions get made where the drought is happening.
  3. Expand water metering and require industry to report its water use — you can’t manage what you don’t measure.
  4. Develop and fund a provincial water storage plan — climate resilience needs infrastructure and natural storage alike.
  5. Modernize industrial water rates — and dedicate the revenue (up to $100M/year) to watershed security and drought preparedness.
“The challenge isn’t a lack of solutions — it’s a lack of delivery.” — Coree Tull, Chair, BC Watershed Security Coalition, World Drought Day 2026
