Why letters work

Your message matters more than you think

Provincial offices track constituent mail by issue and riding. A hundred letters on watershed security lands it in the minister’s weekly summary; a few thousand makes it a mandate item. That’s how “nice-to-have” becomes “must-do” — and it’s why the letter tool is CodeBlue’s front door.
