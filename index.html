The CodeBlue Plan

Our wealth is our water.
CodeBlue has a plan to secure it.

Three moves. None of them radical. All of them overdue — and every one backed by a strong majority of British Columbians.

MOVE 01

Make big industrial users pay their fair share

B.C. charges the lowest industrial water rates in Canada — big industries like fracking and mining pay pennies. Charge the true cost of water, and reinvest every dollar in our watersheds.

MOVE 02

Get tough on water wasters and polluters

Tougher rules, better enforcement, and stronger penalties — because a fine that’s cheaper than compliance is just a licence to pollute.

MOVE 03

Give local people the power to protect their water

Funding, training and tools so communities, First Nations and local governments can restore and manage the water sources they know best.

The movement

You’re in good company.

CodeBlue BC stands with the BC Watershed Security Coalition — a non-partisan alliance of 57+ organizations representing more than 285,000 British Columbians: farmers, hunters and anglers, Indigenous peoples, local governments, businesses and conservationists.

Together we’re calling for a $1-billion Watershed Security Fund, modern industrial water rates, and local watershed boards.
